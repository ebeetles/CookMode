# SPEC.md — Cook Mode

## 1. Overview

Cook Mode is a cooking instruction app that tracks your recipes and instructs you step by step during the cooking process, including inline timers for tasks that need it, as well as nudge your phone to go to the next step without having to touch your phone screen for if your hands are occupied or dirty during the cooking process. It also keeps your phone awake so you don't have to keep having it fall asleep and you having to unlock it over and over again. This app will be useful for people learning to cook.

## 2. Problem

Sometimes while cooking you have a recipe on your phone, you have to keep your phone open on a recipe you found online, then you also want to scroll sometimes but your hands may be dirty from cooking and now you have to go rinse your fingers and dry it just to swipe or unlock your phone and it gets really annoying.

## 3. Platform & constraints

- Expo project generated by `npx create-expo-app@latest` (default template, no `--template` flag).
- Navigation uses Expo Router; all screens live in `src/app/`.
- Use the Expo SDK version listed in `package.json`; no SDK upgrades.
- Must run in **Expo Go** on iPhone 17, iOS 26.6.2. No development builds, no custom native modules.
- No backend, no network requests, no accounts.
- Packages allowed (install with `npx expo install`):
  - `@react-native-async-storage/async-storage`: persistence
  - `expo-keep-awake`: screen stays on in Cook Mode
  - `expo-speech`: read steps aloud (text-to-speech)
  - `expo-haptics`: vibration on timer end / step change
  - `expo-sensors`: accelerometer for nudge-to-advance (in scope, see §5.3)
  - `@expo-google-fonts/barlow-condensed`: display font (see §4 Typography)
- Already in the default template and allowed: `expo-router`, `expo-font`, `@expo/vector-icons` (use Ionicons for all icons), `react-native-safe-area-context`. Animations use `Animated` from `react-native`; no animation libraries.
- Remove the template's example screens and tabs (use `npm run reset-project` if it exists in `package.json`;
  otherwise delete them by hand). The app has exactly three routes: `index`, `add`, `cook/[id]`. Stack navigator with `headerShown: false` on every screen; each screen draws its own top bar.
- Portrait only. Respect safe-area insets on every screen.
- Not allowed: speech recognition / voice commands (requires a dev build), recipe import from URLs.
- Timers only need to run while Cook Mode is open in the foreground. Background timers and push notifications are out of scope.

## 4. Visual design

### Direction
Inspired by kitchen prep labels: masking tape and Sharpie on a white-tiled kitchen.
Browsing screens are calm and cool. Cook Mode is the one bold screen: the whole
screen is tape-yellow with large black "marker" text, readable from about 1 m away.

### Colors (no other colors anywhere in the app)
| Name    | Hex     | Used for |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Tile    | #F3F5F2 | Background of Recipe List and Add Recipe |
| Steel   | #DFE4E1 | Recipe cards, text input backgrounds |
| Sharpie | #1C1F24 | All primary text, icons, Cook Mode progress bar |
| Tape    | #EAD9A6 | Recipe title labels on cards; full background of Cook Mode |
| Basil   | #2F6B4F | Primary buttons (Add recipe, Save recipe, Resume) with white text |
| Flame   | #2F6FDB | Timer chip while a timer is running, white text |
| Alarm   | #C8371F | Timer chip when a timer reaches 0:00, white text |
Secondary text: #4A4F57 on light screens, #5C5232 on Cook Mode.
Remaining progress segments: #C9B985. White (#FFFFFF) only as text on Basil, Flame and Alarm.
Exception: native confirmation dialogs (`Alert.alert`) use system colors.
Light mode only.

### Typography
- Display font: Barlow Condensed (SemiBold 600 and Medium 500), loaded with
  @expo-google-fonts/barlow-condensed. If it fails to load, fall back to the
  system font in bold. The app must not crash or hang.
- Body font: the system font (SF Pro on iOS, Roboto on Android).
- Sizes (pt):
  - Cook Mode step text: 42, Barlow SemiBold, line height 1.1
  - Cook Mode timer: 36, Barlow SemiBold, tabular digits (digits must not shift width as they count down)
  - Screen titles: 30, Barlow SemiBold
  - Recipe card titles: 19, Barlow SemiBold
  - Body / inputs / buttons: 16, system font
  - Secondary text: 13–14, system font
  - Nothing smaller than 13
- Sentence case everywhere. No all-caps text.

### Layout and spacing
- All text is left-aligned, including the Cook Mode step text.
- Spacing uses only these values: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32.
- Screen edge padding: 16.
- Touch targets are at least 48×48 on browsing screens and at least 64×64 in
  Cook Mode (usable with a knuckle).
- No drop shadows. Cards are separated by their Steel fill, not shadows.
- Corner radius: cards and buttons 10; tape title labels 2 (tape has sharp edges).

### Signature elements
- Tape labels: every recipe title on the Recipe List sits on a Tape-colored strip
  sized to the text, like a prep label.
- Cook Mode progress: a row of segments, one per step. Completed and current
  steps are filled Sharpie; remaining steps are a darker tape tone (#C9B985).
- Cook Mode layout, top to bottom: exit (X) on the left, "4 of 7" in the center,
  nudge and read-aloud toggles on the right; progress segments; step text in the
  upper third; timer chip below the step (only if the step has a timer); Back
  button bottom-left with the hint "Nudge or tap for next" bottom-right.

### Motion and feedback
- Advancing a step: the new step slides in from the right (about 200 ms).
  Going back slides in from the left.
- A detected nudge: the screen briefly darkens (about 120 ms) plus a light haptic,
  so the user knows it registered.
- Timer reaching 0:00: chip turns Alarm red and pulses until the user taps it
  or advances; heavy haptic plus spoken "Timer done."
- No other animations.

### Copy
- Buttons say exactly what they do: "Add recipe", "Save recipe", "Start cooking",
  "Resume step 4", "Start over", "Delete recipe", "Cancel", "Back".
- Empty Recipe List: heading "Add your first recipe" with an "Add recipe" button.
- Validation errors appear in Alarm red under the field: "Add a title",
  "Add at least one step".
- Last step done: "That's everything. Enjoy." with a "Back to recipes" button.


## 5. Screens

### 5.1 Recipe List (home): `src/app/index.tsx`

**Top bar:** screen title "Recipes" (30, Barlow SemiBold), left-aligned, 16 from the edges.

**List:** a vertical scrolling list of recipe cards, 12 apart, 16 screen edge padding.
Sort order: newest first (by `createdAt`). A newly saved recipe appears at the top.
Seeded recipes keep the order given in §7 (seed recipe 1 above seed recipe 2).

**Recipe card (collapsed):** Steel fill, radius 10, padding 16, no shadow. Top to bottom:
1. Title on a tape label: Tape strip sized to the text (not full width), padding 4 vertical / 8
   horizontal, radius 2, title 19 Barlow SemiBold in Sharpie, wraps to at most 2 lines.
2. Meta line (14, secondary): `"7 steps · 25 min of timers"`. Use "1 step" for one step.
   If the recipe has no timers, show only `"7 steps"`.
3. Only if the recipe has saved progress: a second meta line `"Paused at step 4 of 7"`.

**Tapping a card** expands it in place (no animation). Only one card is expanded at a time;
tapping another card collapses the first. Tapping the expanded card's title area collapses it.
The expanded card adds, below the meta lines:
- "Ingredients" (16, system font, semibold), then one line per ingredient (16), prefixed "• ".
  If the recipe has no ingredients: "No ingredients listed" (14, secondary).
- Buttons (each at least 48 tall, radius 10, 16 system font):
  - No saved progress: "Start cooking" (Basil, white text, full width). Opens Cook Mode at step 1.
  - Saved progress at step N: "Resume step N" (Basil, full width), and below it "Start over"
    (transparent with a 2 Sharpie border, Sharpie text, full width). "Resume step N" opens Cook
    Mode at step N. "Start over" sets progress to step 1 and opens Cook Mode at step 1.
  - "Delete recipe": text-only button (Sharpie text, underlined), at least 48×48, left-aligned,
    last item in the card.

**Delete:** tapping "Delete recipe" shows a native confirmation `Alert` titled
`Delete "<title>"?` with "Cancel" and "Delete" (destructive style). "Delete" removes the recipe and
its saved progress from storage and the list immediately. "Cancel" does nothing. No undo.

**Add button:** when at least one recipe exists, a full-width "Add recipe" button (Basil, white
text, at least 48 tall) is pinned to the bottom of the screen, 16 from the edges, above the safe-area
inset. The list scrolls behind it with enough bottom padding that the last card is fully visible.
It opens Add Recipe.

**Empty state** (zero recipes): no list and no pinned button. Instead, at the top under the screen
title: heading "Add your first recipe" (30, Barlow SemiBold), then 16 below it an "Add recipe"
button (Basil, white text, at least 48 tall). It opens Add Recipe.

**Loading:** until storage has been read, show only the Tile background (don't flash the empty
state).

### 5.2 Add Recipe: `src/app/add.tsx`

**Approach: (a) multiline boxes with inline timers.** No per-step rows.

**Top bar:** "Cancel" text button on the left (at least 48×48, Sharpie, 16). Below it, screen title
"New recipe" (30, Barlow SemiBold). "Cancel" returns to the Recipe List and discards anything typed,
with no confirmation. The Android back gesture does the same.

**Fields** (top to bottom, in a scroll view that keeps the focused field above the keyboard).
Labels 16 semibold in Sharpie, inputs Steel fill, radius 10, padding 12, 16 system font,
at least 48 tall:

| Field | Input | Required | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title | single line, max 60 characters | yes | trimmed; whitespace-only counts as empty |
| Ingredients | multiline, at least 4 lines tall, grows | no | one ingredient per line; each line trimmed; blank lines dropped |
| Steps | multiline, at least 6 lines tall, grows | yes, at least 1 | one step per line; each line trimmed; blank lines dropped |

Helper text under Ingredients (13, secondary): "One per line."
Helper text under Steps (13, secondary): "One step per line. Add a timer at the end, like (10 min)."

**Timer parsing** (per step line, after trimming):
- A step has a timer if the line *ends* with a parenthetical matching this regex
  (case-insensitive): `\((\d{1,3})\s*(min|mins|minute|minutes)\)\.?$`
  (an optional period after the closing parenthesis is allowed, e.g. `Simmer (10 min).`)
- The number must be 1–180. If it is 0 or above 180, the line is treated as plain text
  with no timer and the parenthetical stays in the text.
- When a timer is parsed, the parenthetical (plus any whitespace before it and the optional period
  after it) is removed from the step text. Example: `Simmer the sauce (10 min)` → text `Simmer the sauce`, timer 10 minutes.
- At most one timer per step; only the parenthetical at the end of the line counts.
- If removing the parenthetical leaves empty text (the line was just `(5 min)`), the step text
  becomes `Wait` with the 5-minute timer.

**Save:** a full-width "Save recipe" button (Basil, white text, at least 48 tall) under the
Steps field. It is always enabled. On tap:
1. Validate. If the title is empty, show "Add a title" under the Title field. If there are no
   non-blank step lines, show "Add at least one step" under the Steps field. Both can show at once.
   Errors are 14 system font in Alarm red, 4 below the field. Nothing is saved and the screen stays
   open.
2. An error disappears as soon as the user edits that field.
3. If valid: save the recipe to storage, then return to the Recipe List, where the new recipe is
   at the top (collapsed).

**Editing an existing recipe is out of scope.** To change a recipe, delete it and add it again.

### 5.3 Cook Mode: `src/app/cook/[id].tsx`

The whole screen is Tape. Layout follows §4 "Cook Mode layout".

**Top bar** (one row, 64 tall):
- Left: exit button, Ionicons `close`, 64×64.
- Center: `"4 of 7"` (16, system font, semibold, Sharpie).
- Right: two toggles, 64×64 each, 8 apart: nudge (Ionicons `hand-left`) then read-aloud
  (on: `volume-high`, off: `volume-mute`).
  Toggle **on**: icon in Tape on a Sharpie-filled square, radius 10. Toggle **off**: Sharpie
  icon, no fill. Accessibility labels: "Nudge to advance, on/off", "Read aloud, on/off".

**Progress segments:** under the top bar, a row of equal-width segments, one per step, 4 apart,
8 tall, radius 2. Steps 1 to current: Sharpie. Remaining: #C9B985.

**Step area:** everything between the progress segments and the bottom row.
- Step text: 42 Barlow SemiBold, line height 1.1, left-aligned, starting in the upper third of
  the screen. Long text shrinks to fit (`adjustsFontSizeToFit` with `numberOfLines={6}`, `minimumFontScale`
  0.6), never scrolls. (`adjustsFontSizeToFit` has no effect without `numberOfLines`.)
- Timer chip (only if the step has a timer): 24 below the step text, left-aligned, at least 64
  tall, radius 10, padding 12 horizontal, time in 36 Barlow SemiBold with tabular digits, white
  text. See Timers below.

**Bottom row:** "Back" button bottom-left (at least 64×64, 2 Sharpie border, radius 10, Sharpie
text 16). It is **not shown on step 1** (its space stays empty). Bottom-right: hint text (14,
#5C5232): `"Nudge or tap for next"` when nudge is on, `"Tap for next"` when nudge is off.

#### Navigation
- **Next:** a single tap anywhere in the step area (except the timer chip) advances one step.
  So does a nudge (see below).
- **Back:** the Back button goes back one step.
- Step changes animate per §4 (new step slides in from the right on next, from the left on back,
  200 ms). Taps and nudges during the 200 ms animation are ignored, so one action never moves two
  steps.
- Each step change plays a light haptic (`Haptics.impactAsync(Light)`), once.
- Progress (current step) is saved to storage when Cook Mode opens and on every step change. So
  opening a recipe and exiting on step 1 leaves the card showing "Paused at step 1 of N".
- **After the last step:** advancing from the last step shows the finish view on the same
  screen: all segments Sharpie, text "That's everything. Enjoy." (42 Barlow SemiBold), and a
  "Back to recipes" button (Basil, white text, at least 64 tall). No Back button, no hint, no
  timer. Nudges and taps on the background do nothing here. The recipe's saved progress is deleted
  as soon as the finish view appears. "Back to recipes" and the X both return to the Recipe List.
- **Exit mid-recipe:** the X (or Android back) returns to the Recipe List immediately, no
  confirmation. Progress stays saved, so the card shows "Paused at step N of M".
- If the recipe `id` doesn't exist (e.g. deleted), show "Recipe not found" (30 Barlow) and a
  "Back to recipes" button.

#### Read aloud (`expo-speech`)
- A toggle, **default on**. The setting is global (not per recipe) and persists across launches.
- When on, each step is spoken when it appears (including step 1 on entering Cook Mode, and on
  going back). Spoken text: the step text, then if the step has a timer, `"Timer, 10 minutes."`
  (`"1 minute"` for 1). Any speech in progress is stopped first (`Speech.stop()`), so steps never
  overlap.
- The finish view speaks "That's everything. Enjoy." when read aloud is on.
- Turning the toggle off stops speech immediately. Turning it on speaks the current step.
- **Repeat:** long-press (500 ms) anywhere in the step area re-speaks the current step, even if
  read aloud is off, with a light haptic. A long press does not advance.
- Leaving Cook Mode stops speech.

#### Timers
- A step's timer **starts automatically** when the step appears, at its full duration.
- Display: `m:ss` (e.g. `9:05`); `h:mm:ss` at 60 minutes or more. Tabular digits: use
  `fontVariant: ['tabular-nums']`; if Barlow's digits still shift width, render each character in
  a fixed-width box. The colon must not move as the time counts down.
- Remaining time is computed from a stored end timestamp (`endAt = Date.now() + duration`), not by
  decrementing a counter, so it doesn't drift. Update the display at least 4 times per second.
- **Chip states:**
  - Running: Flame background, white time.
  - Done (reaches 0:00): Alarm background, shows `0:00`, pulses (opacity 1 → 0.5 → 1, 1000 ms loop).
    At the moment it hits 0:00: one heavy haptic (`Haptics.impactAsync(Heavy)`) and the app speaks
    "Timer done." This alert is spoken **even if read aloud is off.**
  - Dismissed: tapping the chip while it pulses stops the pulse; it stays Alarm red showing `0:00`.
- Tapping the chip while running does nothing (no pause in v1) and does **not** advance.
- **Leaving a step with a running or done timer cancels it.** No alarm fires later. Returning to
  that step (Back, or Next again) restarts its timer from full duration.
- Exiting Cook Mode cancels the timer.
- If the app is backgrounded mid-timer: no guarantees while in the background. On return, the chip
  shows the correct remaining time (because of `endAt`); if 0:00 passed while away, the done alert
  fires on return.

#### Screen
- `useKeepAwake()` in the Cook Mode screen: the screen never dims or locks while Cook Mode is open.
  It is released when Cook Mode unmounts, so the phone returns to its normal auto-lock on the Recipe
  List and Add Recipe screens.

#### Nudge-to-advance (in scope, `expo-sensors` Accelerometer)
- A toggle, **default on**. Global setting, persists across launches.
- Subscribe to the accelerometer at a 50 ms interval only while Cook Mode is open, nudge is on, and
  the step view (not the finish view) is shown. Unsubscribe otherwise.
- **Flat check:** the phone counts as flat when, averaged over the last 500 ms, `|z| ≥ 0.9` g and
  `|x| ≤ 0.25` and `|y| ≤ 0.25`. A nudge can only fire if the phone has been flat continuously for
  at least 1000 ms. That stops it firing while the phone is held, and for the first second after
  setting it down.
- **Bump:** a nudge fires when a single sample's magnitude differs from 1 g by more than 0.3 g
  (`|sqrt(x²+y²+z²) − 1| > 0.3`) while the flat check passes.
- **Cooldown:** after a nudge, ignore further bumps for 1500 ms.
- **Touch suppression:** ignore bumps from the moment any finger touches the screen until 500 ms
  after the last finger lifts (track with a touch-start/touch-end handler on the screen's root view).
  Pressing a button or the screen jolts a flat phone, and without this a press on Back could fire a
  nudge and move *forward*.
- On a nudge: the screen briefly darkens (Sharpie overlay at 20% opacity fading to 0 over 120 ms),
  one light haptic, and the app advances one step (the step-change haptic is not played a second
  time). A nudge also dismisses a pulsing timer, since advancing cancels it.
- Put all thresholds (0.9, 0.25, 0.3, 500 flat window, 1000, 1500, 500 touch suppression, 50) in one file, `src/constants/nudge.ts`,
  so they can be tuned after testing on the phone.

## 6. Data & persistence

AsyncStorage, JSON values, all keys prefixed `cookmode:v1:`.

| Key | Value | Written when |
|---|---|---|
| `cookmode:v1:recipes` | `Recipe[]` | a recipe is saved or deleted; seeding |
| `cookmode:v1:progress` | `{ [recipeId]: stepIndex }` (0-based) | Cook Mode opening; every step change in Cook Mode; "Start over"; deleted on finish and on recipe delete |
| `cookmode:v1:settings` | `{ readAloud: boolean, nudge: boolean }` | a toggle changes |
| `cookmode:v1:seeded` | `"true"` | right after the seed recipes are first written |

```ts
type Step = { text: string; timerMinutes: number | null };
type Recipe = {
  id: string;            // Date.now().toString(36) + random suffix; no uuid package
  title: string;
  ingredients: string[];
  steps: Step[];         // at least 1
  createdAt: number;     // ms since epoch
};
```

- Each recipe keeps its own progress, so several recipes can be paused at once.
- **Running timers are not restored.** Resuming opens the saved step and restarts its timer from
  full duration.
- **Missing or unreadable data** (key missing, `JSON.parse` throws, or the value has the wrong
  shape):
  - `recipes` missing and `seeded` missing → first launch: write the seed recipes, then set `seeded`.
  - `recipes` missing or unreadable but `seeded` is `"true"` → start with an empty list. Never
    reseed. The bad value is overwritten on the next save.
  - `progress` unreadable → treat as `{}`. Progress for a recipe that no longer exists is ignored.
    A saved step index past the last step is clamped to the last step.
  - `settings` unreadable → defaults (`readAloud: true`, `nudge: true`).
  - Storage errors never crash the app. Log them with `console.warn`.
- **Fonts:** render the app once Barlow Condensed has loaded *or* failed. If neither has happened
  after 3 seconds, render anyway with the fallback (system font, bold). Never hang on the splash
  screen.

## 7. Seed data

Preload these on **first launch only**, in this order. If the user deletes one, it stays
deleted (see `cookmode:v1:seeded` in §6). Their `createdAt` values must keep recipe 1 above
recipe 2 in newest-first order.

**Seed recipe 1: Egg fried rice**
- Ingredients:
  - 2 cups cooked rice, cold (day-old is best)
  - 3 eggs
  - 2 green onions, sliced, whites and greens separated
  - 2 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
  - 2 tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 tsp sesame oil
  - 2 tbsp neutral oil
  - Salt and white pepper
- Steps (one line each in the Steps box; the numbers are for reference, not typed):
  1. Break up the cold rice with your hands so there are no clumps.
  2. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt.
  3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or large pan over high heat until it shimmers.
  4. Scramble the eggs until just set, then move them to a plate.
  5. Add the rest of the oil, the garlic and the green onion whites. Stir for 30 seconds.
  6. Add the peas and carrots and stir until hot (2 min)
  7. Add the rice, press it flat against the pan, let it sit, then toss. Keep repeating (4 min)
  8. Add the soy sauce, sesame oil, eggs and green onion greens. Toss to combine.
  9. Taste, add salt or white pepper if needed, and serve.

**Seed recipe 2: Jammy soy eggs**
- Ingredients:
  - 4 large eggs, straight from the fridge
  - 1/2 cup soy sauce
  - 1/2 cup water
  - 2 tbsp sugar or honey
  - 1 clove garlic, smashed
  - Ice
- Steps:
  1. Bring a medium pot of water to a rolling boil.
  2. Mix the soy sauce, water, sugar and garlic in a container until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Fill a bowl with ice and cold water.
  4. Gently lower the eggs into the boiling water with a spoon (7 min)
  5. Move the eggs into the ice bath (5 min)
  6. Tap each egg all over and peel it under running water.
  7. Put the eggs in the marinade, cover, and refrigerate for at least 2 hours.

Stored form: recipe 1 has 9 steps, 2 with timers (6 min total). Recipe 2 has 7 steps, 2 with
timers (12 min total).

## 8. Out of scope

- Voice commands / speech recognition
- Importing recipes from URLs or photos
- Accounts, cloud sync, sharing
- Background timers and notifications
- Editing recipes (delete and re-add instead)
- Timers that keep running after you leave their step; more than one timer at a time
- Pausing or restarting a timer by hand
- Viewing ingredients inside Cook Mode (they're on the expanded card before you start)
- Ingredient scaling, unit conversion, servings
- Photos, search, tags/categories, reordering recipes
- Undo for delete; "discard changes?" confirmation on Add Recipe
- Dark mode, landscape, tablet layouts
- Timers in seconds (only whole minutes)

## 9. Acceptance criteria

Each is checked on the phone in Expo Go. "Force-quit" means swiping Expo Go away in the app
switcher, then reopening the project. The criteria are run in order on one fresh install (later
criteria use the "Test" recipe created in 7). To get a fresh install: on Android, Settings → Apps →
Expo Go → Clear storage; on iPhone, delete and reinstall Expo Go and sign back in to the same Expo
account.

1. **First launch:** on a fresh install, the Recipe List shows exactly two recipes, "Egg fried
   rice" above "Jammy soy eggs", each title on a tape-colored strip. Their meta lines read
   "9 steps · 6 min of timers" and "7 steps · 12 min of timers".
2. **Expand:** tapping a card shows its ingredients and a "Start cooking" button. Tapping a second
   card collapses the first.
3. **Delete:** expanding "Jammy soy eggs", tapping "Delete recipe" then "Delete" removes it from the
   list. After a force-quit it is still gone, and it doesn't come back.
4. **Empty state:** after deleting every recipe, the screen shows "Add your first recipe" and an
   "Add recipe" button, and no pinned bottom button.
5. **Validation, both fields:** on Add Recipe, tapping "Save recipe" with every field empty shows
   "Add a title" under Title and "Add at least one step" under Steps in red. The screen stays open
   and the list doesn't change.
6. **Validation, whitespace:** a title of only spaces, with steps of only blank lines, shows the
   same two errors. Typing in Title makes "Add a title" disappear right away.
7. **Successful save:** title "Test", steps `Chop onion` and `Simmer (1 min)` → "Save recipe"
   returns to the Recipe List, with "Test" at the top showing "2 steps · 1 min of timers".
8. **Persistence:** after step 7 and a force-quit, "Test" is still at the top with the same meta
   line.
9. **Start and first step:** "Start cooking" on "Test" opens a yellow screen showing "1 of 2", the
   first segment dark, the step text "Chop onion", and no Back button.
10. **Forward and back:** tapping the step area shows "Simmer" with "2 of 2", sliding in from the
    right. Tapping "Back" returns to "Chop onion", sliding in from the left.
11. **Finish:** on the last step, tapping the step area shows "That's everything. Enjoy." and
    "Back to recipes". Tapping it returns to the list, where the card shows no "Paused at step"
    line.
12. **Read aloud:** with read aloud on (the default), each step is spoken when it appears. Tapping
    the read-aloud toggle mid-sentence stops speech at once, and later steps aren't spoken. After a
    force-quit, the toggle is still off. Long-pressing the step area speaks the current step.
13. **Timer start and finish:** reaching "Simmer" shows a blue chip reading "1:00" that starts
    counting down right away. At 0:00 the chip turns red and pulses, the phone vibrates once, and
    the app says "Timer done." even with read aloud off. Tapping the chip stops the pulse.
14. **Tabular digits:** while the timer counts down from 1:00 to 0:50, the colon doesn't move
    sideways (check with a screen recording).
15. **Skipping a running timer:** going Back from "Simmer" with 0:40 left, then waiting 60
    seconds, gives no vibration and no "Timer done." Tapping forward again shows the chip at 1:00.
16. **Resume:** open "Test", advance to step 2, then force-quit. On reopening, the card reads
    "Paused at step 2 of 2". Expanding it shows "Resume step 2" and "Start over". "Resume step 2"
    opens Cook Mode on "Simmer". "Start over" opens it on "Chop onion".
17. **Keep-awake:** with auto-lock set to 30 seconds, Cook Mode left untouched for 3 minutes doesn't
    dim or lock. After tapping X, the Recipe List left untouched dims and locks within about 30
    seconds. *Note:* Expo Go may keep the screen awake on every screen during development. Check
    first by leaving the Recipe List open with auto-lock at 30 seconds; if it never dims there,
    this criterion is checked in code instead: `useKeepAwake()` is called only in
    `src/app/cook/[id].tsx`.
